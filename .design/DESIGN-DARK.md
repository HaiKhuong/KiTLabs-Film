## Brand & Style

This design system synthesizes high-concept mythic cultivation with futuristic cinematic technology. It envisions an ethereal, supreme-tier streaming interface that feels like handling an artifact from an astral realm—blending ancient imperial luxury with neon cyber-talismans.

### Aesthetic Foundation
- **Style Mix:** Dark Glassmorphism infused with Celestial Luxury. Translucent obsidian interfaces, ethereal micro-refractions, and boundless negative space simulate floating through a digital void.
- **Tone & Mood:** Mystical, authoritative, hyper-premium, and transcendent. The interface minimizes functional clutter so that film posters, celestial trailers, and curated lore become radiant projections within an obsidian void.
- **Key Characteristics:** 
  - Rim-lit golden auras and spirit-energy glows replacing hard divider lines.
  - Razor-sharp typographic hierarchy matching high-end festival curation and imperial decrees.
  - Fluid, meditative micro-transitions that evoke shifting astral mist.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from cosmic void matter, celestial gold treasures, and energized spiritual talismans.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#D4AF37` / `#FFE088`):** Celestial Champagne Gold. Reserved for primary actions, critical status crowns, spotlight badges, and supreme tier identifiers.
- **Secondary (`#00E5CC` / `#6FD8C8`):** Spirit Talisman Cyan. Used for streaming telemetry, playback progression, interactive energy pings, and high-tech cultivation indicators.
- **Surfaces & Backgrounds:**
  - Base Void: `#0C0E14` (Deep obsidian black).
  - Elevated Plate: `#121620` (Dark slate, 60% opacity with backdrop blur).
  - Floating Sanctuary: `#181D2A` (Rich indigo slate for active cards and popovers).
- **Text & Foreground:**
  - Pure Light: `#FFFFFF` (Headings, primary hero typography).
  - Starlight Muted: `#A4B0C6` (Metadata, body prose, durations).
  - Astral Shadow: `#5A667E` (De-emphasized labels, inactive indicators).

## Typography

The typography pairs grand, literary, imperial drama with modern, ultra-legible digital ergonomics.

### Hierarchy & Treatment
- **Display & Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Evokes ancient scroll manuscripts and monumental cinematic titles. Used for feature film banners, collection spotlights, and section headers. High contrast between thick and thin strokes creates prestige.
- **Interface & Readout (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Ensures flawless legibility at fast scanning speeds across devices. Used for content descriptions, technical stream parameters (4K HDR, Dolby Atmos, Realm classification), timestamps, and interaction buttons.
- **Letter Spacing:** All uppercase labels, category pills, and metadata utilize extended tracking (`0.08em` to `0.1em`) to mimic celestial sigil inscriptions.

## Layout & Spacing

The streaming architecture operates on a 12-column fluid grid on desktop and a flexible 4-column structure on mobile, prioritizing cinematic aspect ratios (16:9 for hero stages, 2:3 for celestial scrolls/posters, and 21:9 for immersive banner previews).

### Breakpoints & Adaptive Rhythm
- **Desktop (1200px+):** Outer margins set to `3rem` (`space-xl`), gutters at `1.5rem`. Hero carousels extend full viewport bleed with dark gradient feathering.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** Margins contract to `2rem`, gutters maintain `1rem`. Secondary sidebar collapses into floating glass navigation.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** Margins set to `1.25rem`, gutters at `1rem`. Grids simplify to horizontal scroll rails with partial peek previews (1.2 cards visible) to prompt horizontal discovery.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is defined through light emission, spiritual radiance, and layered atmospheric obsidian glass rather than physical drop shadows.

### Glassmorphic Layers
- **Plane 0 (The Astral Void):** Pure background canvas (`#0C0E14`), anchor point for non-interactive views.
- **Plane 1 (Spirit Glass Panels):** `rgba(18, 22, 32, 0.65)` layered with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`. Rimmed with a hairline top border: `linear-gradient(90deg, transparent, rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2), transparent)`.
- **Plane 2 (Floating Sanctuary):** Modals, playback control overlays, and active cards at `rgba(24, 29, 42, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(32px)`.

### Celestial Lighting & Luminescence
- **Gold Astral Glow:** Applied to primary focus states, VIP badges, and active selections using diffused colored glow: `0 0 28px -4px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.28)`.
- **Cyan Talisman Energy:** Active playback sliders, audio visualizer pulses, and live stream markers emit a sharp spiritual aura: `0 0 16px 0 rgba(0, 229, 204, 0.45)`.
- **Borderless Isolation:** Standard physical card borders are avoided. Edge definition relies on subtle gradient lighting and micro surface-color variance.

## Shapes

The design uses soft, refined corners (`roundedness: 1` — 0.25rem base, scaling up to 0.5rem for cards and modal frames). This retains the disciplined, crisp edges of ancient jade slabs and cutting-edge cinematic monitors while avoiding organic or playful circularity.

- **Media Tiles & Posters:** `0.5rem` (`rounded-lg`) corner radii to maintain tight, architectural grids.
- **Pills & Status Glyphs:** Geometric capsules with sharp edge discipline; status badges use angled cut motifs or disciplined soft radii (`0.25rem`).
- **Interactive Control Knobs:** Small circular anchors (`0.5rem`) enclosed in tight geometric track envelopes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Heavenly Seal):** Solid champagne gold (`#D4AF37`) gradient fill transitioning to `#FFE088`, crisp black typography (`#0C0E14`, weight 700). On hover, blooms an ambient gold aura (`0 0 24px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary (Talisman Ghost):** Translucent glass base (`rgba(18, 22, 32, 0.6)`), 1px gradient hairline border (`#D4AF37` to `#00E5CC`), text in gold. Hover initiates a gentle cyan back-glow.
- **Icon / Player Actions:** Pure circular obsidian discs with 1px gold hairline borders and cyan-illuminated playback symbols.

### Movie & Series Cards
- **Borderless Glow Cards:** Full-bleed artwork framed without hard borders. Hover state scales the card by `1.03` with a soft gold outer bloom (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)`), revealing an obsidian glass overlay at the foot with metadata, sound specs, and watch-action glyphs.
- **Progress Track:** Integrated hairline progress bar along the lower card edge in `#00E5CC` with active cyan particle glow.

### Chips & Badges
- **Cultivation Realm Badges (VIP / 4K Ultra / Master Cut):** Slender glass chips with `#FFE088` text, an ambient gold inner glow, and a micro gold jewel icon.
- **Spirit Filter Chips:** Dark slate glass pills. Unselected: `#5A667E` text. Selected: `#0C0E14` text on cyan spirit talisman background with cyan drop-glow.

### Form Inputs & Search Bar
- **Astral Inscription Bar:** Sleek glass channel (`rgba(18, 22, 32, 0.7)`), inset placeholder text in `#5A667E`. Active focus creates a dual-edge cyan-to-gold hairline border and displays search suggestions inside a frosted glass floating realm.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Spiritual Node Checkboxes:** Square anchors with `0.25rem` corners, filled with obsidian slate. Checked state reveals an electric cyan checkmark energized with a soft `#00E5CC` talisman glow.
- **Realm Radios:** Concentric circle system; selected state anchors a solid gold center jewel enclosed within a floating cyan aura ring.

### Playback HUD (Player Controls)
- Fully transparent gradient scrim fading into deep obsidian at the bottom.
- Scrubber rail styled as a spirit meridian line: inactive line in `#181D2A`, buffered line in `#5A667E`, and played portion in `#00E5CC` terminating in a diamond-shaped celestial cursor.
