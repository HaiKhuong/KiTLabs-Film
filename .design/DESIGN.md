## Brand & Style

This design system synthesizes traditional Xianxia metaphysics—transcendence, golden cores (Jindan), ethereal jade talismans, and celestial palaces—with ultra-modern cybernetic precision and streaming entertainment luxury. The aesthetic represents "Digital Cultivation" (Cyber-Xianxia): a celestial streaming terminal constructed from light, gold, and jade crystal.

The visual style merges clean, high-luminance light glassmorphism with delicate metallic filigree, auric illumination, and sacred digital talismanic geometry. The user experience evokes serenity, unhurried divine status, and pristine cinematic fidelity. The canvas avoids dark cyberpunk grit in favor of luminous transcendence: pure snow backdrops, crystalline frosted panels, radiant golden halos, and razor-sharp typographic discipline.

## Colors

The color palette is anchored in divine luminance, metallic alchemical resonance, and ethereal silk jade accents.

- **Primary (`#D4AF37` — Champagne Gold):** Serves as the golden core of the UI. Applied to primary calls-to-action, playback timelines, membership badges, and divine state accents.
- **Secondary (`#2A9D8F` — Ethereal Silk Jade Slate):** Evokes ancient jade slips, meditative stillness, and spirit roots. Applied to secondary tags, quality badges (4K Ultra / Master Audio), filter states, and active meditative indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#B8860B` — Radiant Amber Gold):** Used for intense interactive feedback, auric highlights, VIP tier indicators, and glow drop-shadows.
- **Neutral (`#2D3142` — Platinum Obsidian Gray):** Supplies ink-like contrast against the ethereal snow surfaces. Body copy renders at `#64748B`, while container boundaries utilize crisp platinum frosted borders (`#E2E8F0`).

Surfaces are dominated by pure snow variants: `#FFFFFF` for primary cards, `#FAFAFA` for canvas backgrounds, and `#F5F6FA` for elevated floating pods.

## Typography

The typographic pairing reflects celestial literature translated into digital architecture:
- **Playfair Display (Headlines & Cinema Titles):** Imparts the timeless prestige of ancient scripture scrolls and imperial cinematic halls. Its high stroke contrast creates instant luxury on pristine backgrounds.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Body & UI Copy):** Delivers clean readability, balanced apertures, and smooth geometric clarity for plot synopses, actor metadata, and navigation links.
- **Space Grotesk (Labels, Technical Badges & Daoist Metrics):** Provides geometric precision for technical movie attributes (e.g., `IMAX ENHANCED`, `AUDIO: DAO-DOLBY 7.1`, timestamps, chapter runes, bitrate counters). It represents the digital talisman engine of the experience.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an expansive 12-column fluid grid system engineered for cinematic immersion. 

- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12 columns, 3rem margins, 2rem gutters. Layout allows sweeping hero spotlights, multi-row category carousels, and persistent floating navigational seals.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8 columns, 2rem margins, 1.5rem gutters. Secondary rail collapse into smooth horizontal scroll lists.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4 columns, 1rem margins, 1rem gutters. Horizontal media rows extend bleed-to-edge with trailing snap cards.

The rhythm emphasizes breathing space and intentional stillness. Content blocks never crowd one another; generous vertical gaps ensure each cinematic title acts as an illuminated altar piece.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses ethereal multi-layered glassmorphism accented by auric luminescence rather than muddy dark shadows.

- **Base Canvas:** Solid snow white (`#FAFAFA`) overlaid with ultra-subtle sacred talismanic geometric watermarks rendered at 2% opacity in champagne gold.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Containers & Rails):** Translucent snow glass (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)`) with a `16px` backdrop-filter blur and a crisp `1px` border of `rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`.
- **Tier 2 (Interactive Floating Cards):** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)` with `24px` backdrop blur, a hairline border of metallic gold (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)`), and an ambient soft halo: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12), 0 4px 12px rgba(45, 49, 66, 0.04)`.
- **Tier 3 (Active Player Modals & Divine Focus States):** Full optical clarity `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)`, framed in polished gold chrome border (`1px solid #D4AF37`) with radiant amber auric glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 35px rgba(255, 215, 0, 0.35), 0 20px 40px rgba(45, 49, 66, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The design uses balanced, modern curvature (Level 2). Standard UI cards, input enclosures, and buttons feature `0.5rem` (8px) corner radiuses, escalating to `1rem` (16px) for major modal panels and cinema viewport frames. 

To evoke talismanic precision, pill geometries (`rounded-full`) are reserved exclusively for tags, micro-badges, audio chips, and primary play pill buttons. Subtle 45-degree chamfer accents may be introduced on decorative thumbnail corners to hint at digital jade seals.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Golden Transcendence):** Solid linear gradient (`135deg, #FFD700 0%, #D4AF37 100%`) with white/platinum ink typography (`#2D3142`), bold weights, `0.5rem` radius, and an ambient auric glow. Hover state intensifies the glow radius and applies a subtle luminous shimmer.
- **Secondary (Jade Seal):** Frosted glass surface (`rgba(42, 157, 143, 0.08)`), border `1px solid rgba(42, 157, 143, 0.35)`, text in Jade (`#2A9D8F`). Hover transitions to solid tint with white text.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Pure transparent backdrop, champagne metallic text (`#B8860B`), bottom underline rendered as an ethereal talismanic line rule that animates outward on hover.

### Cinema Cards & Poster Containers
- Poster cards feature an aspect ratio of 2:3 or 16:9 for widescreen features.
- Posters sit in a frosted container with `1px` border (`#E2E8F0`). On hover, the card scales up subtly (1.03x), the border turns into Champagne Gold (`#D4AF37`), and a delicate Daoist cyber-rune watermark glints in the top-right corner alongside a golden aura glow.
- Movie details reveal from bottom-up through a jade-tinted frosted gradient scrim.

### Chips & Badges
- Built using `Space Grotesk` uppercase typography with `0.08em` letter spacing.
- Compact padding (`0.25rem 0.65rem`), rounded pill shape.
- Color variations: Golden Immortal (VIP / 4K Master), Jade Slip (Genre / Spiritual Affinity), and Slate Platinum (Runtime / Year).

### Form Inputs & Search Bar
- Floating frosted omnibar with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` background and `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`.
- Left-adorned with a minimalist golden compass/divination icon; placeholder copy in muted slate (`#64748B`).
- Active focus state activates a perimeter gold ring with ambient amber bloom and no harsh browser default outlines.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Filters
- Filter tabs utilize pill selectors with sliding golden-liquid indicators.
- Checkboxes feature rounded squares (`4px` radius) with an ethereal jade checkmark indicator and a metallic gold border when checked.

### Player HUD (Celestial Cinema Console)
- Floating glassmorphic dock suspended over playback video.
- Scrubber bar features a glowing golden thread with current buffer displayed as translucent jade silk. The scrubber head is an amber-cored digital bead that pulses slightly during seek states.
